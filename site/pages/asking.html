Editing by asking

You edit by saying what you want in the chat. The AI watches your footage, plans the cuts and makes them, step by step, while you watch. Ask the way you would ask a video editor.

Things to ask

Cut and shape

"Cut this into a 30 second trailer." "Remove the silences and the retakes." "Make it vertical for TikTok and keep my face in frame."

Captions and text

"Add bold captions, two words at a time." "Put a title at the start: City Lights." "Add my name as a lower third when I first appear."

Music and sound

"Cut the shots to the beat of this song." "Clean up my voice." "Add a whoosh on each transition."

Color and look

"Give it a warm film look." "Match the second clip to the first." "Make the sky more dramatic."

Effects and motion

"Zoom in on the punchline." "Add slow motion when the ball lands." "Make it snow."

Objects and people

"Blur the background behind me." "Remove the sign on the wall." "Put this logo on the phone screen."

The full list of what the AI can do is on All tools.

Tips for good results

  • Say what it is for. "An Instagram Reel for a coffee shop, upbeat, under 20 seconds" gives the AI more to work with than "make it good".
  • Change things one at a time. After the first cut, ask for small changes: "shorter intro", "bigger captions", "less music under my voice".
  • Let it show you. Ask "show me a frame at 10 seconds" or "render a preview" to see before you decide.
  • Choose when it asks. For a narration voice, music or a look, the AI may offer a few options with a play button or a picture. Pick one, or just type what you prefer.

Your own files

Mention footage, music, photos or a script you have, and the AI finds them: "use my beach videos from last summer", "add the song in Downloads", "follow the script in my Documents".

  • It searches your usual Videos, Pictures, Music, Downloads, Desktop and Documents folders, and any folders you add in Settings, Memory.
  • It can read text documents there (scripts, notes, subtitles) and copy media into the project. Nothing outside the project is ever changed, moved or deleted.
  • PDF and Word files cannot be read yet; save a script as text (.txt or .md) to use it.

Plans first

Turn on Plan next to the model picker (or ask "plan it first") and the AI looks at your project, then proposes a plan instead of editing. Nothing changes until you press Approve. Press Change it to say what to do differently. For big requests the AI may propose a plan on its own.

Keep talking while it works

You do not have to wait. Send a message while the AI is working ("also add captions", "make it shorter") and it shows in the chat as usual. The AI reads it as soon as it finishes the step it is on and goes on with it in mind; its reply continues under your message. If you stop the AI first, your message goes with your next request.

Long conversations and your credit

Every request reads the whole conversation, so a long one costs more each time. VideditBridge keeps that in check:

  • Caching: the parts that repeat from step to step are read from the provider's cache, which costs a fraction of the normal price.
  • A limit you choose: Settings, AI models, Context limit (100k tokens unless you change it). From half of it, a line above the chat box shows how much is left.
  • Summaries: at the limit, older messages are summarized by the provider's cheapest model and the conversation goes on from the summary. A line in the chat marks where. Press Summarize now to do it earlier, or turn off Summarize automatically to decide yourself. Your project is never affected.

Memory and instructions

  • Instructions (Settings, Memory): what the AI should always do, for example "keep videos under 60 seconds" or "my brand colors are navy and orange". They go with every request, with every model.
  • What it remembers about you: as you work, the AI saves lasting preferences, like a caption style you like. They show in Settings, Memory, where you can remove any.
  • What it remembers about a project: what the video is for, who is in it, decisions made. They show at the top of the project's Summary tab.

Point at things

  • A clip, text or sound: click it on the timeline strip under the preview, then ask "make this shorter". The request is about what you picked.
  • A spot in the picture: click in the preview, then ask "put the title here" or "remove this".
  • Files: drag them onto the chat, press +, or paste them with Ctrl + V: a copied file, a copied picture or a screenshot becomes an attachment. Pictures you attach are shown to the AI as well.

Undo, history and export

  • The arrows at the top undo and redo. History on the left lists every change.
  • Review lists what a viewer might notice: text over a face, clipped highlights, banding, and more.
  • Export (top right) saves the finished video. You can also ask: "export in 4K".
  • Projects save as you go. Each conversation is kept with its project; the clock icon above the chat lists them.

Tabs and the menu

Open several projects at once, each in its own tab. The arrow button at the top left (or Ctrl+Shift+A) opens the tab search: type to find a tab or a recently closed project, and reach Help and guides and Settings.
